FriendlyFeathers Application

Generic social networks and pet apps focus on dogs and cats, the avian community suffers from a Specialized Knowledge Gap. FriendlyFeathers centralizes verified avian medical resources, adoption and community engagement into a single, trusted ecosystem designed to lower the barrier to expert bird care.

Overview

FriendlyFeathers is a comprehensive ecosystem designed to solve the fragmentation in bird ownership. While dogs and cats have endless resources, bird owners struggle to find specialized medical care and verified information. I designed a dual purpose platform that merges High Stakes Utility (Vet Finder & Adoption) with Community Engagement (The Birddex & Social Feed).

The Problem

Bird owners often rely on unverified social media groups for life saving medical advice because specialized apps don't exist.

Role

UX designer, UI designer, full time

Tools

Identifying the Market Gap

The Fragmented Experience:

Bird owners currently rely on generalized platforms for critical care advice. These spaces are broad, unverified and not designed for species specific health needs. Life saving medical guidance is buried between memes and misinformation.

The Expertise Void:

Unlike dogs and cats, birds lack a centralized digital ecosystem. There is no dedicated platform that connects bird owners directly to certified avian veterinarians, ethical rescues and structured educational resources in one cohesive environment.

The Trust Gap:

Specialized apps do not exist, bird owners default to peer advice over professional guidance. This creates risk during medical emergencies and delays access to proper care.

The Adoption Disconnect:

Bird adoption and rehoming processes are scattered across local classifieds and informal groups, with little transparency, vetting, or support for responsible ownership.

Identity

Voice / Color Palette / Font Style / Components

I developed the FriendlyFeathers identity to balance warmth with credibility. Soft, approachable visuals and breathable layouts create a sense of safety and compassion, while structured typography and clean UI patterns reinforce trust and authority. Social features are intentionally designed to feel curated and purposeful, supporting meaningful connection without overwhelming the experience. The result is a focused ecosystem where community and expert guidance coexist seamlessly.
