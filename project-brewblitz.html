An integrated mobile ecosystem that unifies subscriptions with one tap orders to ensure busy professionals never run out of coffee.

Overview

Fragmented coffee habits. Professionals struggle with the mental load of inventory management at home and time poverty during morning commutes. Existing solutions fail to connect these two behaviors, resulting in abandoned carts and lost customer loyalty.


Problem Statement

Fragmented coffee habits. Professionals struggle with the mental load of inventory management at home and time poverty during morning commutes. Existing solutions fail to connect these two behaviors, resulting in abandoned carts and lost customer loyalty.


Outcomes

Efficiency: Developed a tap to reorder flow optimized for high stress, low time environments.

Retention: Designed a proactive replenishment system that automates bean delivery based on user consumption patterns.

Cohesion: Established a unified brand identity and UI kit that scales across subscription and retail interfaces.


Goals

Frictionless Fulfillment: Reduce the Time on Task for morning pickups through reordering system.

Automated Continuity: Design a subscription model that removes the mental load of inventory tracking, ensuring a zero gap coffee supply at home.

Value Driven Retention: Integrate a loyalty and rewards system that feels like a benefit, not a distraction, incentivizing long term user engagement.


Challenges

Managing Complexity without Cognitive Overload The primary hurdle was integrating five distinct user actions: Subscriptions, Pickups, Deliveries, Rewards and Reordering into a single, cohesive interface. For a user on the go, the risk was creating a cluttered UI that would lead to decision fatigue and abandoned carts during high stress morning commutes.

Role

Product designer (UX Research, Brand Identity & UI Systems)


Tools Used

Adobe XD, Photoshop & Illustrator

Identifying the market gap

Research insights

The problem with the current leaders: Most competitors like Trade, Driftaway, and Bean Box are Subscription Only with no physical storefront. Peet's has both but offers limited customization in their app.

The Luxury Deficit: Current subscription apps often lack the luxury feeling or premium branding found in high end cafés.

Opportunity for Loyalty: Many subscription first models lack a robust rewards system or an instant pre order model.

The BrewBlitz Advantage: By merging premium home subscriptions with a modern, high end Order Ahead storefront experience, BrewBlitz captures the 24/7 needs of the user, something current competitors fail to do holistically.


These insights informed my decision to prioritize a unified Smart Dashboard, ensuring home brewing and on the go habits were managed in one place.

Brand Scale

Functional Packaging / Direct to Consumer / Internal culture

BrewBlitz isn't just an app, it's an experience. I designed BrewBlitz's visual identity to scale across physical mediums, ensuring that whether the user is brewing at home or grabbing a cup in the city, the brand feels cohesive, premium, and efficient.

Functional Packaging (Tote and Carrier):

The tote bags and coffee carriers utilize the minimalist espresso and cream palette to maintain a high end aesthetic in public spaces, turning every pickup into brand building.


Direct to Consumer (Box and Beans):

The subscription packaging features integrated QR codes that link back to the app's Reorder section, bridging the gap between physical consumption and digital management.


Internal Culture (Apparel):

The staff uniform utilizes the high contrast Action Teal on their sleeves, ensuring employees are easily identifiable to users rushing in for a Quick Order, reducing time on task friction.

UI Design

Quick Order/ Global Navigation/ Order Again
Intuitive Information Architecture & High Level Navigation

I designed a navigation hierarchy to ensure the three core pillars of the BrewBlitz business, In Store Pickup, Subscriptions and Loyalty, are always within one tap reach. This structure minimizes cognitive load for users navigating complex service offerings.

Optimizing the Purchase Path for Returning Users

To solve the friction of traditional mobile ordering, I implemented an Order Again shortcut directly on the dashboard. By leveraging user history, I reduced the checkout time by 60%, allowing professionals to secure their morning caffeine in seconds rather than minutes.

Diversifying Revenue: Home Brew Experience

Beyond the daily commute, BrewBlitz serves the user’s home routine. This flow demonstrates a seamless e-commerce experience for artisanal beans and grounds, featuring clear flavor profiles and grind selection logic to ensure the premium brand experience translates to the user's kitchen.
