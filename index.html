About me

Hi, I'm Tracy.

I'm a UX/UI designer with a fine arts background. I design digital products end to end: research, wireframes, prototypes and high fidelity UI, with a strong focus on usability and visual clarity. My background in fine arts (BFA, Painting and Drawing) gives me an edge; a trained eye for composition, color theory and visual hierarchy that makes interfaces usable and genuinely engaging.

My process centers on understanding the user first, then designing solutions that are both functional and visually intentional. I'm comfortable owning a project from low fidelity to polished, interactive prototypes and I pay close attention to detail, a habit built from years of fine art practice, where precision and craft aren't optional.

As a multidisciplinary designer with a focus on visual storytelling and a strong foundation in drawing, painting and user empathy. I blend artistry with strategy to create impactful, user centered designs. I thrive at the intersection of art and usability.

Tools I love: Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, and a well worn sketchbook. I'm currently seeking UX/UI design roles and internships where I can bring both strategic thinking and strong visual craft. If you’re crafting something meaningful and need a designer who leads with intention and craft, let’s connect via email or linkedin.
