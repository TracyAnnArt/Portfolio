About me

Hi, I'm Tracy.

I’m a product designer with a BFA in Painting and Drawing and a growing practice in digital design. I bring an artist’s eye for composition and visual hierarchy to product problems, connecting research, interaction design, and polished UI.

I enjoy making complex ideas easier to navigate—whether that means mapping a flow, prototyping an interaction, or shaping a visual system. My work spans mobile and web products as well as brand identity.
